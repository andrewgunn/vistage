A proven, complete path to success

Six parts that work together, so every month moves you forward.

It starts with the group

Once a month, meet 12-16 high-calibre executives from non-competing organisations. They can test solutions in ways your direct reports can't.

Your Chair guides discussions using a proven framework, and meets you one-to-one for executive coaching on your challenges and opportunities.

A model CEOs have relied on for decades

It started simply. In 1957, businessman Bob Nourse brought together a handful of executives to help each other solve their business challenges. He made sure they came from non-competing industries, kept conversations confidential and brought in experts to share their insight.

That formula grew into an international community of 45,000 members, and we still hold to Nourse's vision: to help high-integrity leaders make great decisions that benefit their companies, families and communities.
