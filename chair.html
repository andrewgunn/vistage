What you need to know

How does Vistage work?

This isn't a standard networking group. It's a confidential forum of experienced executives committed to helping each other solve hard problems, weigh opportunities and build strategies for better performance.

Whether the question is operational, financial, structural or personal, the group brings wisdom, perspective and accountability to help each executive make better decisions.

Who are Vistage members?

Managing directors, business owners and key executives of small and medium-sized companies with £5M-£50M in annual revenue, from nearly every industry. This diversity lets us build groups of non-competing leaders with relevant expertise.

What are the member benefits?

Members take part in monthly, Chair-led private advisory board meetings and get one-to-one executive coaching, expert speakers, online networks and exclusive content. As a result, they make better decisions, improve their work-life balance and grow their companies 2.2 times faster than the average SME.

Who are Vistage Chairs?

An elite group of independent contractors who build and run private advisory groups of high-calibre leaders from non-competing industries. Ideal Chairs have 15+ years of executive experience, strong financial acumen, excellent management skills and an entrepreneurial spirit.

What are the Chair's responsibilities?

With Vistage's support, Chairs recruit members to launch their first group, then grow and sustain it to up to 12 members. Each month they lead a candid, confidential roundtable and meet every member one-to-one for tailored coaching.

What are the basic requirements?

Successful Chairs are usually former or transitioning business leaders or chairs with at least £5M of P&L responsibility. They have an entrepreneurial spirit, a passion for mentoring, experience leading high-performing teams, strong networks, the ability to listen to and challenge senior executives, and a drive to give back.

Is Vistage a franchise?

No. Chairs are independent contractors running their own Vistage Chair practices. There's a one-off fee of £3,000 that covers all training and resources to start your practice. Vistage provides the support, learning and development, best practice and a framework for success.

How much will I earn?

As an independent contractor, you decide the scope of your practice. If you move forward, we'll discuss the full earning opportunity with you.

How will Vistage help me build my groups?

We invest heavily in new Chairs: an award-winning learning and development programme, a support team in the office and in the field, marketing campaigns, infrastructure and the strength of the Vistage brand.
